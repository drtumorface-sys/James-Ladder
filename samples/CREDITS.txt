James' Ladder — instrument sample credits
=========================================

The app loads an instrument's samples only when it is chosen. Each folder holds
single-note recordings, one every two to four semitones; the app re-pitches them
to fill the notes between. Changes from the originals: a subset of notes was
selected and the files were converted to MP3 (by the Tone.js and
tonejs-instruments projects, from which these copies were taken).

piano/  — Salamander Grand Piano
  Author:  Alexander Holm
  Source:  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
           (MP3 copies: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio, folder "salamander")

  Derived from the sfz instrument in the sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
  GitHub repository.

guitar/ — Acoustic guitar (steel string)
  Source:  University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios,
           Musical Instrument Samples — https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
           (MP3 copies: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments,

  Terms:   "may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions."
