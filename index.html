Harmony Check

Checks your progression against five rules of common-practice harmony — tonic framing, subdominant order, no dominant retrogression, correct use of vii°, and a proper cadence. A high score means the progression follows textbook voice-leading; a low one just means it breaks the rules, which is sometimes exactly what you want.
