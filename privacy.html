James' Ladder

Privacy policy, support & credits · last updated 9 November 2026

James' Ladder collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your sketches stay on your device.

What is stored, and where

The app saves your work so it is still there next time you open it:

  • Sketches: your chord progressions, the keys you opened, capo, tempo and drum beat.
  • The drum machine pattern you last used.

All of this is kept in the app's storage on your device. The app never uploads it, and nobody else, including the developer, can see it.

On Android, if you have turned on your phone's backup to your Google account, Android may include the app's storage in that backup, so your sketches can move to a new phone. That backup is yours, managed by Android; the developer can't see it.

To delete it, remove sketches from ▤ Sketches in the app, or uninstall the app (on the web, clear this site's data in your browser). Uninstalling or clearing site data removes everything, and it can't be recovered.

What the app sends over the internet

Nothing about you. The app, its fonts and its 3D library are all bundled with it, so it works offline. When you use the web version, your browser downloads the app's files from GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs for that, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Permissions

The app needs no special permissions: no microphone, camera, location or contacts. It only plays sound.

In the Android app the instrument recordings are built in. On the web, choosing Piano, Guitar or Nylon downloads that instrument's recordings from the same site the app comes from, once. They are then kept for offline use.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if a feature such as cloud sync is added, this page will be updated first, and nothing new will be collected without telling you in the app.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: open an issue at github.com/drtumorface-sys/James-Ladder/issues.

When reporting a problem, it helps to say which device and browser (or app version) you're using and what you tapped just before it happened.
